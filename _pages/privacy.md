## Privacy Policy

**Obsidian Calendar Sync** is a personal, single-user application. It is not distributed to the public and has exactly one user: its author.

### What the application does

The application connects to Google Calendar in order to read, create, and edit calendar events inside the author's personal knowledge base (Obsidian). It runs locally on the author's own computer.

### What data is accessed

Only the data belonging to the author's own Google Account, within the scopes the author grants at the moment of authorization:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events` — to create and modify events
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly` — to read events

### How data is stored

Calendar data is retrieved directly from Google's servers and cached locally on the author's own machine. There is no server, no database, and no third party in between. No data leaves the author's computer.

### Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. No advertising or analytics services are involved.

### Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access immediately stops the application from reading or writing calendar data.
